# Code of conduct

Aubergine is made by and for people who work at a terminal all day. Everyone taking part, in issues, discussions,
pull requests and reviews, is expected to keep it a place where anyone can ask a question or send a change.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with someone new.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Say what you think and why, and listen to the answer.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Credit others' work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Continuing anything above after being asked to stop.

## What happens

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people from the repository
when this is not followed. To report something, use **Report content** on the comment or issue, or write to the
maintainers through a private [security advisory](https://github.com/thirstyeggplant/aubergine/security/advisories/new)
if it should not be seen by others. Reports are kept private.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
